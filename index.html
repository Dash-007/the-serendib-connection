About Us

Bridging Sri Lankan Culture Across the Pacific

The Serendib Connection is a non-profit dedicated to bringing world-class Sri Lankan music, culture, and sports to Northern California — uniting our community through unforgettable shared experiences.

Born from the teams behind the sold-out WAYO and Gypsies events, we're building a sustainable platform for premium cultural productions.

Our Purpose

We Exist To

  • Organize Sri Lankan musical, cultural, and sports events in Northern California
  • Connect Sri Lankans across generations and backgrounds
  • Support artists and cultural ambassadors from Sri Lanka
  • Build a long‑term, sustainable platform for high‑quality community events
We believe culture is more than entertainment — it's a shared identity that brings people together.
